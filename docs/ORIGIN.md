# Origin

Store Ops Incident Board is part of the Kinetic Gain atlas expansion into Food / Restaurant Tech.

The repo exists to make a specific commercial and operator story visible:

- store incidents are not just support tickets
- labor recovery and dispatch parity create real guest-promise risk
- reopen packets need governed evidence before demand returns
- franchise and marketplace posture break when recovery workflows drift

This surface turns those operational problems into one buyer-readable control plane with named owners, required evidence, and recovery timing.
