# Kinetic Gain Embedded tie-back

Store Ops Incident Board demonstrates a Food / Restaurant Tech workflow that fits naturally into Kinetic Gain Embedded.

## Why the primitive matters

Restaurant and food-commerce products often need to surface:

- store incident events
- recovery blocker states
- reopen packet posture
- publish-safe decision notes

That information is valuable inside customer-facing or operator-facing product surfaces, but it cannot depend on unsafe direct-write access or disconnected spreadsheets.

## Embedded extension

Kinetic Gain Embedded turns this primitive into:

- in-product dashboards for incident and guest-promise trust
- buyer-safe analytics around staffing, dispatch, and reopen posture
- tenant-aware operator workflows that preserve evidence and review timing
- security-first integrations that let restaurant platforms expose recovery posture without exposing unsafe back-office mutation paths

## Commercial fit

This is especially relevant for:

- multi-location restaurant platforms
- dispatch and delivery orchestration products
- franchise support and field-ops systems
- store recovery and labor operations products
